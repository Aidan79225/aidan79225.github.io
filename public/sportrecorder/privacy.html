Privacy Policy — SportRecorder

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the SportRecorder mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information the App handles

The App lets you record fasting/eating activity. It handles the following data that you create:

  • Eating records — the date and time of each eat event.
  • Photos — pictures you choose to take and attach to an eat record (stored as compressed images).
  • Location (GPS) — if you grant location permission, the approximate coordinates at the moment you create an eat record, attached to that record.
  • Notes — any text you choose to write on an eat record.
  • Fasting & reminder settings — your selected fasting/eating schedule, your own custom fasting windows, and your reminder preferences (lead time, quiet hours, and which reminders are enabled).
  • Google account e-mail — only if you turn on Backup & restore (see section 4). It is shown in the App so you can see which account your backups go to.

2. How the App uses this data

The data is used solely to provide the App's features to you — showing your fasting timer and history, listing your eating records, displaying the photos and location you attached, and — only if you turn it on — backing your records up to your own Google Drive so you can restore them on a new device.

3. Where data is stored

By default, all data stays on your device. Eating records, notes, settings and location coordinates are stored in a local database on the device, and photos are stored in the App's private folder on the device.

If you turn on Backup & restore, a copy of that data is uploaded to your own Google Drive — see section 4 for exactly what is uploaded and how to delete it. Backup is off until you sign in yourself; the App never backs up on its own.

In both cases the App does not send your data to us, and does not share it with any third party. We do not operate any server that receives your data — backups go directly from your device to your Google account — and the App contains no advertising or analytics tracking.

4. Google account data and Drive backup (optional)

Backup & restore is optional and off by default. It only starts working after you sign in with Google and grant access yourself.

What the App asks for

The App requests a single Google Drive scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This scope can only read and write a hidden folder that belongs to this App (Drive's "application data folder"). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive. The App requests no other Google scope.

What is uploaded

  • Your eating records — date and time, notes, and location coordinates if you attached them.
  • The photos attached to those records.
  • Your custom fasting windows, fasting/eating settings and reminder preferences.

All of it goes into that hidden folder in your Google Drive. It is your data in your account; we have no access to it.

Your Google account e-mail

After you grant access, the App reads the e-mail address of the signed-in account from Google Drive so it can show you which account is in use. It is displayed in the App only. The access token the App receives from Google is used for that session's upload or download and is not stored by the App.

Limited Use

SportRecorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature you asked for. It is never sold, never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, and never read by a human.

5. Permissions

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — used only to tag an eat record with where it was created. It is optional; if you deny it, the record is still saved without a location.
  • Camera — when you choose "Add photo", the App opens your device's camera so you can take a picture for the record.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+) — used to show the fasting reminders you turn on. It is optional; if you deny it, the App simply shows no reminders.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, Android 12+) — used to deliver your reminders at the scheduled time.
  • Run after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — used to restore your scheduled reminders after the device reboots.
  • Network access (INTERNET) — used only by Backup & restore, to talk to Google Drive. If you never turn backup on, the App makes no network requests.

Reminders are generated and scheduled entirely on your device from records and settings you have already entered. Enabling them sends no data off the device.

6. Data retention

Data on your device is retained for as long as you keep it — that is, until you delete it within the App, or until you uninstall the App. We do not retain copies of your data, because we never receive it.

If you use Backup & restore, the App keeps the three most recent backups in your Google Drive and deletes older ones automatically. Those backups stay in your Google account until you delete them (see section 7) — uninstalling the App does not remove them.

7. How to delete your data

You are in full control of your data and can delete it at any time:

  • Delete a single record: on the Record screen, long-press an eating record and confirm. This permanently deletes that record together with its attached photos and location.
  • Delete everything on the device: uninstall the App, or clear the App's storage from your device Settings (Settings → Apps → SportRecorder → Storage → Clear data). This permanently removes all records, photos, location data and settings from your device.
  • Delete your backups from Google Drive: the backup folder is hidden, so it is not listed among your normal Drive files. Delete it from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → SportRecorder → Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke the App's access to your Google account: at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also sign out inside the App, on the Backup & restore screen. Revoking access stops further backups; it does not delete backups already made, so do that separately if you want them gone.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at aidan79225@gmail.com.

隱私權政策 — SportRecorder

最後更新日期:2026 年 9 月 21 日

本隱私權政策說明 SportRecorder 行動應用程式(以下稱「本應用程式」「我們」) 如何處理您的資料。使用本應用程式即表示您同意本政策。

1. 本應用程式處理的資料

本應用程式供您記錄斷食/進食情況,會處理以下由您建立的資料:

  • 進食紀錄 — 每次進食的日期與時間。
  • 照片 — 您選擇拍攝並附加到進食紀錄的相片(以壓縮後的影像儲存)。
  • 位置(GPS) — 若您授予定位權限,則在您建立進食紀錄當下的概略座標,並附加於該筆紀錄。
  • 備註 — 您選擇為某筆進食紀錄寫下的文字。
  • 斷食與提醒設定 — 您選擇的斷食/進食時間方案、您自訂的斷食時段,以及您的提醒偏好設定 (提前時間、勿擾時段,以及各項提醒的開關)。
  • Google 帳號電子郵件 — 僅在您開啟「備份與還原」時(見第 4 節)。它會顯示在應用程式中, 讓您知道備份存到哪一個帳號。

2. 資料的用途

這些資料僅用於提供本應用程式的功能 — 顯示您的斷食計時與歷史、列出進食紀錄、顯示您附加的照片與位置, 以及在您主動開啟的情況下,將您的紀錄備份到您自己的 Google 雲端硬碟,讓您能在新裝置上還原。

3. 資料儲存位置

預設情況下,所有資料都只保存在您的裝置上。進食紀錄、備註、設定與位置座標儲存在裝置的 本機資料庫,照片則儲存在裝置上本應用程式的專屬資料夾。

若您開啟「備份與還原」,這些資料的副本會上傳到您自己的 Google 雲端硬碟 — 上傳內容與刪除方式詳見第 4 節。在您親自登入之前,備份不會啟用;本應用程式不會自行備份。

無論哪一種情況,本應用程式都不會將您的資料傳送給我們,也不會分享給任何 第三方。我們沒有任何接收您資料的伺服器 — 備份是由您的裝置直接送往您的 Google 帳號 — 本應用程式也不含 任何廣告或分析追蹤。

4. Google 帳號資料與雲端硬碟備份(選用)

「備份與還原」是選用功能,預設為關閉,必須由您親自以 Google 登入並授權後才會運作。

本應用程式請求的權限範圍

本應用程式只請求一個 Google 雲端硬碟權限範圍: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata。 此範圍只能讀寫一個屬於本應用程式的隱藏資料夾(雲端硬碟的「應用程式資料夾」), 無法看見、讀取或變更您雲端硬碟中的任何其他檔案。本應用程式不會請求其他 Google 權限範圍。

上傳的內容

  • 您的進食紀錄 — 日期與時間、備註,以及您有附加時的位置座標。
  • 附加於這些紀錄的照片。
  • 您自訂的斷食時段、斷食/進食設定與提醒偏好。

以上全部存放於您自己的 Google 雲端硬碟中的那個隱藏資料夾。那是您帳號裡的您的資料, 我們無法存取。

您的 Google 帳號電子郵件

在您授權後,本應用程式會向 Google 雲端硬碟讀取已登入帳號的電子郵件地址,用以顯示目前使用的是哪個帳號, 且僅顯示於應用程式內。本應用程式自 Google 取得的存取權杖僅用於該次上傳或下載,不會被保存。

Limited Use(有限使用)聲明

SportRecorder 對於自 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與轉移,均遵循 Google API Services User Data Policy,包含其中的 Limited Use 要求。這些資料僅用於提供您所要求的備份與還原功能, 不會被販售、不會轉移給任何其他人、不會用於廣告,也不會有人閱讀。

5. 權限

  • 位置(ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)— 僅用於為進食紀錄標記建立地點。此權限為選用;若您拒絕,紀錄仍會在沒有位置的情況下儲存。
  • 相機 — 當您點選「新增照片」時,本應用程式會開啟裝置相機讓您為紀錄拍照。
  • 通知(POST_NOTIFICATIONS,Android 13 以上)— 用於顯示您開啟的斷食提醒。此權限為選用;若您拒絕,本應用程式將不會顯示提醒。
  • 精準鬧鐘(SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM,Android 12 以上)— 用於在預定時間準時送出您的提醒。
  • 開機後執行(RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)— 用於在裝置重新開機後,重新排定您的提醒。
  • 網路存取(INTERNET)— 僅供「備份與還原」與 Google 雲端硬碟通訊之用。 若您從未開啟備份,本應用程式不會發出任何網路請求。

提醒完全由您裝置上既有的紀錄與設定在本機產生與排程,開啟提醒不會將任何資料傳送至裝置以外。

6. 資料保留期間

您裝置上的資料會保留至您不再保留為止 — 也就是 直到您在應用程式內刪除,或解除安裝本應用程式為止。我們不會保留您資料的任何副本, 因為我們從未取得這些資料。

若您使用「備份與還原」,本應用程式會在您的 Google 雲端硬碟中保留最近 3 份備份, 更舊的會自動刪除。這些備份會留在您的 Google 帳號中,直到您自行刪除為止(見第 7 節)— 解除安裝本應用程式並不會移除它們。

7. 如何刪除您的資料

您可完全掌控自己的資料,並隨時刪除:

  • 刪除單筆紀錄:在「Record(紀錄)」畫面長按某筆進食紀錄並確認, 即會永久刪除該筆紀錄及其附加的照片與位置。
  • 刪除裝置上的全部資料:解除安裝本應用程式,或在裝置設定中清除本應用程式的 儲存空間(設定 → 應用程式 → SportRecorder → 儲存空間 → 清除資料),即會永久移除裝置上的所有紀錄、 照片、位置資料與設定。
  • 刪除 Google 雲端硬碟中的備份:備份資料夾是隱藏的,不會出現在您平常的雲端硬碟檔案清單中。 請至 Google 雲端硬碟 → 設定 → 管理應用程式 → SportRecorder → 刪除隱藏的應用程式資料。
  • 撤銷本應用程式對您 Google 帳號的存取權:請至 myaccount.google.com/permissions。 您也可以在應用程式的「備份與還原」畫面登出。撤銷授權只會停止後續備份, 不會刪除已經建立的備份,若要一併清除請另外執行上一項。

8. 兒童隱私

本應用程式並非以未滿 13 歲兒童為對象,也不會在知情的情況下蒐集其資料。
